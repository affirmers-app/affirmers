Before you take part
Not an investment. Buying, holding or trading a coin gives no claim on its fees and no return of any kind. Pool money only pays backers who deliver payments to the recipient, or may go back to Backers if not paid out within 14 days.
Paid in SOL. A verified payment of $P pays P plus 10% in SOL, converted at the SOL/USD price when the payment is verified. That SOL amount is then fixed.
Where the fees go. Each coin's Pump.fun creator fees fund its pool, after 10% goes to Backers. The pool repays backers; fees not paid out within 14 days may go back to Backers.
Strikes. A hold that expires without a receipt, a rejected receipt or cancelling a hold each count as a strike. 3 strikes - 30-day ban; any later ban lasts 90 days.
You pay the recipient yourself in X Money. Backers never moves your dollars and never posts on X for you. Naming a recipient doesn't mean they endorse the coin.
